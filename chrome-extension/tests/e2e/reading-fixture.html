How can I take useful notes without interrupting my reading?
Example answer

Make your notes selective and personal.

You do not need to keep every paragraph. Start with one idea that changes how you think or gives you something practical to try.

Keep the sentence that matters, then add why it matters to you.

A useful annotation connects an idea to your next action. Write a question, capture a connection, or mark something to verify later.

Review your collected notes after reading, while the context is still fresh.

Small, thoughtful notes are easier to revisit than a folder full of complete conversations.
